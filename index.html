— Our Story —

More Than Just Coffee

Coffee Clan was born from a simple idea: that a great café should be more than a quick stop for coffee. It should feel like a second home — a place where you can catch up with friends, lose track of time over board games, or simply unwind with a perfectly brewed cup.

Tucked in Punit Nagar, Vadodara, we serve handcrafted beverages, comfort food, and genuine warmth. Whether you're here for the Hazelnut Cappuccino, the Exotic Maggi, or just the vibe — you're always welcome at the Clan.
